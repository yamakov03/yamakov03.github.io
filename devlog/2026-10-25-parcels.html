Parcels

Oct 25, 2026

I'm making a city builder that doesn't have the issues of the leading builders, like Cities: Skylines. I don't want demand that fights the city I'm actually building, traffic that ignores lanes, goods that never really get delivered, or a city that becomes unplayable once it gets big. I want 60 fps, and I want to be able to model a city up to Tokyo.

A parcel is a lot. It has an owner, zoning, a building, and a road it faces. People live there, jobs are there, trucks deliver there, pipes serve it. If a number shows up in the panel, I should be able to click through to the household, the lot, or the money behind it.

The title bar still says Ground Truth. That's what I called it in the design notes. The name is Parcels.

Right now I can paint lots, draw roads, zone them, and run the day. Power, water, sewage, schools, clinics, a developer that fills empty lots, and buses are in. The city in these screenshots is small and it's holding 60 fps. I want that at the size of Tokyo too.

Lots

Parcel 8 is a shop downtown. 1,690 m², zoned High, and it's already as tall as that zone allows. 12 of 24 jobs are filled, stock is 8. The panel says the jobs are underfilled, and that it can't go higher because the zone max is already High. It can develop because it has a road and live power.

Zoning is what you're allowed to build. I can paint homes, shops, industry, or mixed. If I drag onto the same zone next door, they merge. A lot is ready to build once it touches a road and has power. Green outline means ready, amber means I only claimed part of the lot, red means something is blocking it. This map has 8 lots that can build, 4 of them low density, none mid or high yet.

Empty lots get something on them when a developer looks at them, from households, rent, and whether a firm would actually open there. Parcel 2 is homes, 1,455 m², 3 households, density Low, and the zone goes up to High. Moving it to Medium needs four checks, and all four pass: power, water, and sewage are on, a school and a clinic can reach it and still have a seat, and at least half the jobs nearby are filled. The next developer pass should raise the density. No issues on the lot. This run is day 1, morning commute.

Traffic

The blue lines are trips, from where they start to where they need to end. Under that, roads are lanes and the turns those lanes allow. I can mark a one-way or ban a turn. The path has to use a turn the lanes actually have. If a link is loaded, that becomes delay, and that delay is part of the cost of driving, so the next person might walk or take the bus instead.

The road I had selected here had 0 walkers and 44 drivers. The legend marks roads free, busy, or jammed.

Utilities

Power and water are networks, and sewage is the third. A plant has a capacity, and some of it gets lost along the way, so a lot can be out even when the plant is running. Clinic 2 has 120 seats, none taken, and a 200 m radius. 7 parcels are inside it. A homes lot won't upgrade unless a clinic and a school can actually take someone from there.

Buses

A bus line is stops snapped to the road, and the bus drives the streets between them. The cyan line is one route, stops 1 through 8. The overhead shot is the same thing as a map: stop numbers, direction along the curb, and a second color where another route shares the street. Buses have a capacity. Walk, drive, and bus get compared on how long the whole trip takes. If the bus is slow, people don't take it.

Code

The sim is Rust. It has the households, firms, shipments, lanes, utilities, buses, and the money. If the seed and the inputs are the same, I get the same result. The window sends what I did, like placing a road, setting a zone, or following someone, and reads a snapshot back.

This window is a viewer for working on it. winit, wgpu for the roads and the box buildings, egui for the panels. The buildings are boxes with lit windows, which is fine for now. Unreal is the client I want later, talking to the same sim. I can run the checks with no window.

Far from the camera, people are counts and trips. Near the camera, or if I click one, they're a person going to work, school, a shop, or home. Jobs, goods, and money are tracked either way. Most of the city stays aggregated. The area I'm looking at gets drawn one person at a time, which is how I want 60 fps at Tokyo size.

Viewer: cargo run -p groundtruth-lab. Tests: cargo test -p groundtruth-sim.

So far

Lane tools are one-ways and turn bans. The budget shows income and spending. Shops get restocked by truck. A fuller tax setup, rail, ships, freight contracts, and the Unreal client are later.

For this version I want to follow 10 people and have all 10 actually get there, trace something sold in a shop back to a delivery, change a turn ban and see the routes change, and be able to tell why the budget is off in about a minute.
